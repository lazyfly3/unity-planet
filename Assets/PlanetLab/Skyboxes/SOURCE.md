# PlanetLab selected skyboxes

These assets were selectively imported from the user's local package:

`D:\untiy素材\Unity3D_220种精美高清天空盒_AllSky - 220+ Sky   Skybox Set_V5.2.0`

PlanetLab includes 25 six-sided skyboxes: five candidates for each procedural
planet template. The preset Seed deterministically selects one candidate.
They are used only by the fixed and infinite planar PlanetLab modes; the globe
preview and formal space scenes are intentionally unchanged.

The original package's license and redistribution terms still apply to all
imported textures and materials.
